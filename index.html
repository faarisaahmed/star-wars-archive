For nearly five decades, one saga has spread across generations — from a single desert farm boy to a galaxy of rebels, smugglers, clones, and Jedi.

This archive gathers every film and series recognized as official canon: the nine chapters of the Skywalker Saga, the anthology films that widen the frame, and the animated and live-action series that fill in the spaces between.

Old myths and forgotten timelines are set aside. Only the current, official record remains.
